2025 accomplishments

Momentum we banked

  • Kept CAPTCHA-first voting intact across Feature Lab, plaintext, and arcade flows.
  • Rolled out daily login bonuses and Web5 vault pacing while maintaining ship cadence.
  • Shipped 142 community-chosen features with a median 11.4 hour turnaround.
  • Raised our uptime and observability so we could detect stalled votes within a single reset cycle.

2026 plans

Where we’re steering

  • Push anti-brigading further with lighter-weight Turnstile challenges and anomaly alerting.
  • Ship a public ship-log that pairs commits with end-to-end coverage for every implemented feature.
  • Grow the arcade into a seasonal lab where experiments can be promoted to the main board in one click.
  • Reward voters and creators with new coin sinks and collaborative bounties inside the Web5 vault.
